What worked, what didn't

Checked against all 39 posts, not just the top three.

What worked

  • Live stories beat famous names. All three breakouts referenced something people were already discussing that week. A name on its own caps out near 1,900 views.
  • A sharp claim needs no name at all. "Great salespeople don't use the same pitch for everyone" hit 4,590 views with nobody famous in it.
  • Breakouts keep climbing for weeks. The school post doubled to 79,000 a month after going up. Don't judge a video on its first 48 hours.

What didn't, and open questions

  • Generic advice still plateaus. The floor rose to roughly 2,000 views, but those posts draw almost no likes or comments. Higher reach, thinner engagement.
  • Follower conversion is flat, not broken. Followers and reach both grew about 75%, holding steady near one follower per 1,800 views. The lever is a stronger follow prompt, not more reach.
  • The platform-push theory is mostly dead. India's Got Latent broke out on August 12, three weeks outside the original window.
  • Paid lead-gen was premature. One lead, no conversion. Hold the CTA until the growth phase runs out.
