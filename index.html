What worked, what didn't

Account numbers come from the daily export. Topic comparisons use the 28 posts since August 20, tagged by hand.

What worked

  • Shares carried the breakout. September 5 and 6 brought 5,401 shares, 45% of every share the account has ever had. Likes followed reach; shares created it.
  • The lift held. Daily views after the spike settled at 2.5× the pre-spike level, not back where they started.
  • Indian money and culture stories are the format. The 14 posts on wealth, class and culture since August 20 have a median of 27,500 views, and 8 of them cleared 10,000. The 8 business case studies (Domino's, Crossword, the Kardashians) have a median of 1,840.
  • Breakouts are now routine. 8 of September's 24 posts cleared 10,000 views. Before August 20 it was 4 out of 39.
  • Followers convert better. One follower per 1,540 views, up from one per 2,100 on August 21.

What didn't, and open questions

  • Pure sales advice still stalls near 500. Chris Voss, the Ogilvy post, the sales technique post and the argument post have a median of 515 views, even with a much bigger audience now. The August 14 salespeople post (4,590) is still the only one that broke out.
  • This week is flat. Last 7 days: 134,419 views against 135,894 the week before (−1.1%). The account is holding, and the next step up needs another breakout.
  • Few conversations. 420 comments on 1.15 million views, and the Bigg Boss post holds 191 of them. Ending on a question is the cheapest thing to test.
  • Gaps in the post list. Posts from August 25 to September 2 were not in the latest screenshots, and older posts show August 21 numbers. Account totals are unaffected.
